## Competition Description
Teams were given the profile of a company, and were tasked to craft a 5 year-long Leveraged Buyout Deal. Teams were required to create a financial model for the growth of the company over the 5 years and test out different debt structures in order to maximize IRR.

## What we delivered
- A financial model, tracking the forecasted returns from the LBO deal over the 5-year range.
- 3 separate models for 3 separate cases - a bull market, bear market and base market.
- A sensitivity analysis, testing how much the final IRR changes in response to small changes in debt structure.

## What I did
- Studied PE deals, understood the exact workings of a LBO deal
- Helped design the financial model, specifically, quantifying my teammate's assumptions into growth rates / discount rates
- Helped design a deck of slides for the competition.

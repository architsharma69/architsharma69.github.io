## Competition Description
Teams were required to select a company that they believe would benefit from the global scaling of AI infrastructure. They needed to create a pitch to investors to buy the stock of the company an hold it over a 3-5 year horizon.

## What we delivered
- A full stock pitch of `Modine`, a company specialising in data center cooling.
- A fundamental analysis of `Modine`, using DCF valuation
- A contrarian view on the `Modine` stock, arguing that the market has currently undervalued it.

## What I did
- Carried out Market Research on upstream and downstream AI-linked industries, collating research notes for the team to decide on the industry and company
- Designed and wrote out the investment thesis for our chosen company, using popular models such as Porter's Five Forces and SWOT Analysis.
- Designed a proffesional-looking deck of slides for the submission
